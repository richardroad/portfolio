First light

The first image I made with AI, on DALL·E 2. Rough, strange, and the reason everything else on this site exists.

I was taught to hide the dark parts.
Now I point the camera at them.

Shadow is not the absence of light.
It is where light learns its shape.

Accepting it is the first act of love,
and love is how anything gets made.

The world is stranger and kinder than it looks.
We are not here to watch it. We are here to make it.
