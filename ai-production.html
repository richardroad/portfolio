Two promo films for Avito Real Estate and a test spot for Avito Auto.

As a part of Forest Technologies AI Production, I collaborated with the team to create two engaging promotional videos for Avito Real Estate. Additionally, we produced a test project for Avito Auto, showcasing our creative and technical capabilities in aligning with Avito’s brand style and communication goals. Every step of the project, from concept to final delivery, was meticulously coordinated within our team to ensure high-quality results.

Three scenes animated from scratch, a split-screen opening and native Avito widgets.

We developed and animated from scratch three main, distinct scenes of the video script, as well as the split-screen opening sequence. Special Avito widgets were integrated seamlessly, and we prepared multiple versions of the video (10, 15, and 20 seconds) while optimizing them for different platforms and devices. In addition, we generated vertical formats to ensure the content was fully adaptable across all social and digital channels.
